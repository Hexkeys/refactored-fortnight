HOW TO PLAY

Both players start with 3 health. A chamber contains a randomized mix of live and blank shells. On your turn, choose yourself or your opponent: a live shell removes one health, while a blank does no damage. A blank fired at yourself grants another turn. When the chamber is empty, reload it with a fresh randomized set. Last player standing wins. This is a fictional, turn-based game simulation.
