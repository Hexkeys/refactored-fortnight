HOW TO PLAY

Each player starts with 3 energy points. The server generates a hidden sequence of charged and empty quantum pulses. A charged pulse removes one energy point from the selected target. An empty pulse does nothing; choosing yourself for an empty pulse earns another turn. Reload when the sequence is empty. The last player with energy wins. The server controls the sequence and validates turns.
